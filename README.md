# 🦆 Duck Therapy

**A cozy, healing web sanctuary** designed to bring joy, laughter, and comfort to anyone going through tough times—whether you're in a hospital, recovering at home, or just need a pick-me-up. Built with love, duck energy, and a whole lot of quacks! 💖

---

## ✨ **Features**

Duck Therapy is packed with **15 interactive tabs** to keep spirits high and minds engaged. Perfect for **both adults and kids**!

### 🎮 **Games &amp; Entertainment**

- **🧠 Duck Trivia** – Test yer knowledge with easy, medium, and hard duck-themed questions! Score tracking included.
- **⚖️ Would You Rather** – Hilarious, thought-provoking duck dilemmas (e.g., *"Would you rather have golden feathers or rainbow feathers?"*).
- **🔤 Duck Word Game** – Guess the duck-related word before yer guesses run out! (Think Wordle, but with ducks.)
- **🎭 Memory Game** – Match pairs of duck emojis against the clock! Time tracking and victory messages included.
- **🖱️ Click Joy Game** – Click the duck to fill yer joy meter! Features:
  - Floating heart particles on click
  - Milestone celebrations at 5, 10, 25, 50, and 100 clicks

### 📚 **Content &amp; Inspiration**

- **😂 Jokes &amp; Laughs** – Duck-themed jokes with punchlines revealed on click (or get a random joke!)
- **💭 Wisdom (Quotes)** – Real inspirational quotes from authors, philosophers, and even duck wisdom. *"Quack your way through it! 🦆"*
- **📝 Creative Prompts** – Writing and art prompts to spark creativity (e.g., *"Draw what healing looks like to you."*)
- **🎵 Playlist Recommendations** – Curated links to lo-fi, uplifting, and motivational music (Spotify &amp; YouTube).
- **📰 Funny Feed** – Uplifting articles and memes to keep yer spirits high.

### 🌿 **Wellness &amp; Healing**

- **🧘 Breathing Exercise** – Guided 8-second breathing cycles with a pulsing animation to help ye relax.
- **📋 Bucket List** – Add things to do after recovery. Check off items and watch ‘em turn green!
- **🏆 My Progress** – Track yer healing journey with:
  - Days counter (set a start date!)
  - Achievement badges for milestones (1 day, 7 days, 30 days, etc.)
- **💪 Wellness Tips** – Sleep hacks, mental health advice, and funny health facts (e.g., *"Laughing burns calories!"*).
- **🌟 Affirmations** – Uplifting messages to remind ye of yer strength. Click ‘em for heart particles!

## 🎨 **Design Vibes**

- **🌈 Pastel Gradient Background** – Sky blue to light pink, with a subtle hue rotation animation.
- **🎭 Smooth Animations** – Bouncing headers, waddling duck, pulsing milestones, and floating hearts.
- **🌫️ Cozy Aesthetic** – Blurred backdrop filter, rounded corners, and soft shadows for a warm feel.
- **📱 Fully Responsive** – Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **For Users**

1. **Visit the site**: [Duck Therapy](https://ctrlaltbeard.github.io/duck-therapy/)
2. **Pick a tab** and start exploring! Each tab offers a different way to unwind, laugh, or reflect.
3. **No login needed** – Just click and enjoy!

### **For Developers**

Want to run it locally or contribute?

1. **Clone the repo**:
  ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/ctrlaltbeard/duck-therapy.git
   cd duck-therapy
  ```
2. **Open `index.html`** in yer browser. No server needed—it’s a static site!
